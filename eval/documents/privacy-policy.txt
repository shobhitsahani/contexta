Contexta stores uploaded documents, extracted chunks, and chat history for the account owner. Documents are processed to create embeddings used for retrieval.

Data retention: uploaded files and derived data are retained until the user deletes them or closes the account. Account deletion removes files, chunks, vectors, keys, and chats.

Privacy consent is required before the first upload. The consent records a timestamp and policy version. Users may withdraw consent, which blocks further uploads.

Byok chat sends questions and retrieved chunks to the provider selected by the user, using the user's own API key. The provider's terms apply.

The free tier uses a server key. On the free plan, the embedding and chat provider may use submitted content to improve its products. Sensitive files should not be uploaded to the free tier.
